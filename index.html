Cadence privacy policy

Effective: 17 November 2026

Cadence ("the app", "we") is operated by Krati Jain. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: support@cadenceapp.org.

What we collect

  • Health and cycle data. With your permission, the app reads menstrual-cycle data from Apple Health, such as period days and flow. The app never writes to Apple Health. To show you insights and to share with friends you choose, the app stores a summary of this data (period dates and the phases worked out from them) on our servers. You can turn sharing off in Settings, and revoke Health access at any time in the Health app.
  • Period days you log in the app. If you log your period in the app instead of (or as well as) Apple Health, we store those days and any flow you choose. Only you can see them; friends see the same cycle summary as for Health data. Erasing your cycle data in Settings deletes them.
  • Account information. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier, and your name and email if you choose to share them. If you add a phone number or email in Settings, we store it and send a verification code to confirm it.
  • Profile and social content. Your alias, posts, comments, reactions, boops, friend requests and friends.
  • Contacts (optional). If you allow access, the app checks your contacts on your device to suggest friends. When you look up a full email address or phone number, the app sends a scrambled (hashed) version, not the address itself, to find a matching account. Others can find you this way only if you turn on "Let contacts find me".
  • Reports and blocks. If you report something, we keep your report, the reason, any note you add and a copy of what you reported. We keep a list of the people you block.
  • Device data. A push-notification token, if you allow notifications, and basic technical information needed to run the app.
  • Your AI choices. Whether you allowed AI-written tips and the AI photo style, and when.
  • Date of birth. We ask for it once after you sign in, to check this app is right for you (it's for people 16 and over) and to wish you a happy birthday. Only you can see it. It isn't shared with friends or sent to OpenAI.

How we use it

To show your cycle insights, connect you with friends you approve, send notifications you've allowed, review reports and keep the community safe, and secure your account. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share Apple Health data for advertising or data mining. Cycle details go to an AI service only to write tips for you, and only with your permission.

Who we share it with

  • Friends you approve, and only what you've chosen to share.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us and may use the data only to provide their service:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and storage.
    • Expo and Apple: delivering push notifications.
    • Twilio: sending phone verification codes.
    • Resend: sending email, including verification codes and reports to our reviewer.
    • OpenAI, only if you allow it: for AI-written tips, the name of your current cycle phase, and for suggestions with a friend (only if you both allowed it) both of your phases and how in sync you are; for the AI photo style, your profile photo. We never send your name, email or account details with them, and OpenAI doesn't use what we send through its API to train its models.
  • When required: to comply with the law, or to protect people's safety and the app.

Your choices

  • Revoke Apple Health access in the Health app, turn off cycle sharing in Settings, and manage notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Turn off "Let contacts find me" on the Circle tab to stop others finding you by email or phone.
  • Block people from their profile or from a post or comment; manage blocks in Settings → Blocked people.
  • Turn AI-written tips and the AI photo style on or off in Settings → Privacy and permissions. If you say no, you still get our standard tips and your photo is used as it is.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the data linked to it.
  • Email support@cadenceapp.org to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Keeping your data safe

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with Supabase, where access rules let each person see only their own data and what friends have chosen to share with them.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account, its data is removed. Reports about other people's content may be kept for as long as needed to keep the community safe.

Children

This app is for people 16 and over. If someone enters a date of birth under 16, we don't save it and they can't use the app. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it. If you think a child is using this app, email support@cadenceapp.org.

Health information

This app estimates cycle phases and dates from the data in Apple Health and the period days you log. Estimates can be wrong, and the app isn't a medical device: it doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it isn't birth control or a way to plan a pregnancy. Talk to a doctor or nurse about your health.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and post the new version at this address.
